## What is SendCopy?

SendCopy streamlines your LinkedIn lead generation by using smart AI algorithms. Instead of spending hours manually connecting and messaging prospects, SendCopy automates the entire funnel while keeping your outreach natural and personalized.

## Core Features of SendCopy

* **AI-Powered Personalization:** Generate unique, highly relevant messages for every prospect to increase reply rates.
