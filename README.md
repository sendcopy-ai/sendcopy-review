## What is SendCopy?

SendCopy simplifies **automated LinkedIn outreach** using smart personalization algorithms. Instead of wasting hours on manual connection requests and standard cold messages, SendCopy handles profile prospecting, follow-ups, and engagement tracking while maintaining complete account safety.

---

## Core Features of SendCopy

* **AI-Driven Personalization:** Tailor connection messages and outreach copy based on target prospect profiles to boost reply rates.
* **Automated LinkedIn Campaigns:** Build smart drip sequences, automated follow-ups, and conditional triggers without manual intervention.
* **Safety Throttling & Daily Limits:** Protect your LinkedIn account health with built-in action limits and smart human-like activity pacing.
* **Multi-Account Campaign Management:** Manage multiple team or client profiles from a centralized control dashboard.
* **Conversion Analytics:** Track performance metrics, response rates, and meeting conversions with real-time analytics.
